05 · Section 4.5

Attention & cache footprint

Sparse Attention anchors use grouped-query attention with 64 query heads sharing just 2 KV heads. Because the stack leans on Mamba-2, both attention compute and KV-cache growth are heavily reduced.

Grouped-query attention

64 : 2
Query : KV heads (32:1 sharing)
128
Head dimension

32 query heads share each KV head, shrinking the per-token KV projection to 2×128. Table 1

KV cache vs SSM cache

KV cache — grows with sequence length
Mamba SSM cache — constant per batch element

Per Section 4.5: the 32-bit Mamba cache can be larger than the FP8 KV cache up to ~64K sequence length. The release uses FP16 SSM cache with stochastic rounding. §4.5

09 · Pretraining precision

NVFP4 pretraining

Nemotron 3 Ultra was pretrained in NVFP4 on 20T text tokens with a Warmup-Stable-Decay schedule — described in the report as the largest-scale demonstration of stable, accurate NVFP4 training to date.

Two-phase token budget

Phase 1 · 15T
Phase 2 · 5T
diversity & broad domain coveragehigher-quality refinement

Warmup-Stable-Decay LR

Warmup over 200B tokens to peak LR 2.5e-4; the final 5T tokens use −sqrt decay to 2.5e-6. report

NVFP4 implementation

  • Transformer Engine open-source cuBLAS NVFP4 GEMM kernels for fprop, dgrad, wgrad.
  • E2M1 datatype with two-dimensional block quantization on weights.
  • Random Hadamard Transforms on inputs to wgrad.
  • Stochastic rounding on gradients.
Higher precision kept forthe final 15% of the network (16 layers), Mamba output projections, latent projections, QKV & attention projections, MTP layers, and embedding layers.

12 · Engineering transparency

Training instability

Two divergences during pretraining each appeared as simultaneous increases in training cross-entropy loss and wgrad L2 norm. The report documents cause, mitigation, and open questions.

Divergence 1 · ~8T tokens

resolved

Attributed to reducing local gradient-accumulation precision for the output layer from FP32 to BF16. The MTP-2 loss spiked first. Mitigation: rollback and restore FP32 gradient reduction — training restabilized.

Divergence 2 · ~16T tokens

undetermined

Cause undetermined. Mitigated by starting LR annealing from a 15T rollback; the final training horizon was cut to 20T tokens.

Observed, not proven causalImbalanced / dead experts and residual-stream activation-norm imbalance were observed phenomena during training — reported as correlates, not established causes of divergence.
